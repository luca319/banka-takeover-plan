How things work

Open these when the situation comes up.

  • Staying on top of itA light routine so nothing slips through.

    Steps

    1. Keep one “waiting for” list: who you asked, what, and the date. Chase after two working days.
    2. Save important emails in the shared folder on the same day.
    3. Record key meetings with Granola, then add the action items to your list.
    4. Every Friday, take 10 minutes: tick what’s done, pick next week’s top three, and send your manager a short update.
    5. Once things calm down, tidy the shared folder: one folder per topic, and a short index at the top. The meeting agreed this isn’t urgent.
    6. If you’re stuck for more than a day, ask. Being new is the best time to ask.

    Message to copy

    Your managerTeams, Friday update
    Hi [name], a quick update for this week. Done: [items] In progress: [items] Waiting on: [who, for what] Next week I’m focusing on [top three]. Let me know if you’d like anything prioritised differently. Thank you!

    What
    Test new versions of IM Solution, HLA’s sales app on iPad and desktop, before sales staff get them.
    Why
    Sales staff submit cases through the app, so a broken update stops sales.
    Who
    HLA, bank IT in between, you on desktop, and two bankers on iPad.
    When
    Whenever HLA says a new version is installed.

  • New product launchRisk questionnaire, committee approval, then a memo six months later.
    What
    The steps to launch a new insurance product through the bank.
    Why
    No product launches without approval from the Product Risk Approval Committee (PRC).
    Who
    You, Compliance, Legal for the PDS, Business Finance for projections, IT if the app changes, and HLA for materials and competitor data.
    When
    Months before launch. The post-launch memo is due six months after launch.
    Where
    The RCAQ template and PRC deck in the shared folder.
    How
    RCAQ, then the PRC deck, then approval, then launch, then the six-month memo.

    Steps

    1. HLA shares the product proposal. Compare the benefits, features and pricing, and give feedback.
    2. Fill in the RCAQ: the proposal stage first, then pre-launch. The pre-launch date is your planned launch date. Only some of the 32 pages apply.
    3. Compliance reviews the RCAQ, and Legal reviews the PDS.
    4. Build the PRC deck from the template: product type, customer segment, key features, projections, competitor comparison and risks.
    5. For projections, Business Finance has the template. Agree the targets with HLA. HLA usually provides the competitor comparison.
    6. Present at PRC. Your boss usually presents, and you may join. Standard products get few questions.
    7. HLA handles the filing with BNM. You only need their confirmation email.
    8. After launch, send the announcement to sales. HLA runs the training, and sales staff must pass an assessment. Compliance reviews the training first.
    9. Six months later, write a short memo on cases sold and income, recommending whether to continue. Risk will remind you, and your predecessor has a past example.
